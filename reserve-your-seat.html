How it works

  1. Choose your class. Select a class listed in Square.
  2. Choose your date. See actual bookable sessions and availability.
  3. Reserve securely. Complete the required payment through Square.

Any remaining class balance can be arranged with the studio for payment online or in person.

Cancellation & studio credit

For cancellations made at least 48 hours before class, Leo.art offers a studio-credit voucher for the entire verified amount paid. The credit is valid for 12 months from cancellation. Cancellations made within 48 hours do not qualify under the standard policy. Studio staff currently verify and issue credits manually; Square does not automatically apply these vouchers.
