# Leo.art 12-month voucher expiration

Eligible cancellations (at least 48 hours before class start) receive a pending voucher reference for 100% of verified payments. The voucher expiration date is **12 calendar months after the cancellation date**, stored as `voucherExpiresAt` and returned by `/api/cancel`. Unused credit may be carried forward to other classes before expiry.

**Important:** Square payment verification, voucher activation, credit ledger, redemption, and automatic expiry enforcement are not implemented. Staff must manually verify payments and honor/track expiry; do not treat the pending reference as a spendable voucher. Confirm compliance with local gift certificate and consumer-protection requirements before publishing.
