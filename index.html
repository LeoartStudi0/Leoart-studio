Reservations & flexibility

Plans change. Creativity stays.

Cancel at least 48 hours before your class begins to qualify for a future-class voucher for the entire verified amount you paid, including the non-refundable cash deposit and any balance, as studio credit rather than a cash refund. After studio verification, the voucher is valid for 12 calendar months from the cancellation date and may be applied to future classes. Unused credit can be applied to additional classes before it expires. Cancellations within 48 hours are not eligible for a voucher under this policy. All online and in-studio payments must be verified by the studio before voucher value is issued.

Artist Trading Card Club cancellations are handled directly by the studio until its meeting time is announced.

To cancel or request eligible studio credit, contact Leo.art directly with your Square booking confirmation. Square reservations are not managed by this website.
