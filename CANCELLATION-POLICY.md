# Leo.art cancellation and voucher policy (draft for studio approval)

- Cancellations **at least 48 hours before the scheduled start** of a class are eligible for a future-class voucher equal to the **entire verified amount paid (deposit plus any additional balance)**.
- Cancellations less than 48 hours before class start may cancel their seats but do not qualify for a voucher under this proposed policy.
- The server releases seats immediately when a reservation is cancelled.
- Voucher references are **pending**: they are NOT redeemable or automatically activated. Staff must confirm Square payment and issue credit manually.
- Staff must reconcile both Square and in-studio payments to determine the full voucher value. No monetary credit or refund is issued automatically.
- Artist Trading Card Club has no published start time, so cancellation requests must be handled by the studio.
- This version covers Dec 2026–Feb 2027 in Utah Mountain Standard Time. If extending to daylight-saving dates, implement IANA America/Denver timezone conversion instead of fixed MST.
- Production work still needed: Square webhook verification, staff dashboard, secure customer identity confirmation, automated emails, expiring unpaid holds, and actual voucher redemption/credit ledger.
- The website currently accepts cancellation by reference + email. This is not sufficiently secure for a production payment system; require authenticated or emailed one-time cancellation links before going live.
- Have the studio approve the wording and confirm voucher expiration and transferability, and obtain review of applicable local consumer laws.

- **Voucher expiration:** 12 calendar months from the cancellation date. Unused verified credit remains valid until that date. Studio staff must track the expiration and remaining balance manually.
