# Leo.art full-payment voucher policy

For a cancellation at least 48 hours before class starts, the studio issues future-class credit equal to **all verified payments received for that reservation** (deposit plus any additional balance, whether paid online or in person). Cancellations inside 48 hours do not qualify under the standard policy.

## Operational status

This is a policy and wording update, **not automated Square credit issuance**. Cancellation returns a pending voucher reference, but its value is not known to the server. Staff must verify payments, set the credit amount in their records, and manually apply it to future bookings. A customer must not be told their credit is activated until staff confirms it.

If a future class costs less than the voucher, retain the unused credit in the studio's manual ledger. If it costs more, collect the difference. Confirm any expiration period and local legal requirements before publishing a final policy.
